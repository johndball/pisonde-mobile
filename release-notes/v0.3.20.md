# PiSonde Mobile 0.3.20

Packet altitude now displays meters, feet and miles, retaining the original altitude reference and unchanged stored/exported values.

An independent watchdog in the privileged Wi-Fi broker monitors current-boot journal evidence. Repeated upstream scan/control timeouts or onboard authentication-transmission failures trigger recovery of the affected adapter. Missing coverage, an Internet outage or absence of connected clients does not trigger recovery.

The watchdog has a 90-second startup grace, five-minute per-adapter cooldown and at most three attempts per adapter per hour. A shared supplicant restart requires fresh failure evidence on both adapters after isolated attempts; it is limited to once per 15 minutes and twice per hour. Recovery can briefly disconnect clients. No automatic Pi reboot, USB power cycling, driver unloading or SDR reset is added. Limits/counters apply to the running broker process and reset when it restarts.

Wi-Fi shows recovery state, attempts and the last reason. Update/power interlocks defer recovery. Onboard AP activation is also checked by the broker independently of the receiver application. Profiles, passwords, notes, reception history, retention policy and signing trust are preserved.

The reported Wi-Fi and intermittent SDR/weather startup failures have not been conclusively diagnosed. These recovery paths are validated with fault-injection tests; hardware effectiveness still needs field validation. DFM/RS41 reception remains local-only; APRS and Horus upload paths are unchanged.

All 231 offline regression checks pass; no real Wi-Fi/service/power operations are performed by tests.
