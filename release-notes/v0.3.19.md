# PiSonde Mobile 0.3.19

DFM and RS41 reception is now local-only. Direct weather telemetry uploads to sondehub.org, including development endpoint tests and weather payload formatting, are removed. Local decoding, decimal/DMS coordinates, map links, reception history, notes, CSV and GPX remain available.

On upgrade, old pending weather jobs become disabled without deleting receptions or saved payloads. Enabling uploads again cannot resume them. Historical weather JSONL exports remain available for records saved by older releases; they do not generate or retransmit data.

Horus uploads to SondeHub Amateur and APRS forwarding to APRS-IS are unchanged. Updated screenshots show synthetic demonstration data, colored controls and the dedicated Device controls page.

Signed installation preserves settings/history. Device-side RF checks remain separate from offline regression tests.
